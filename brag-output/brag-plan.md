# brag plan — Titluri de Stat România

**What it is:** A free site charting every Fidelis & Tezaur government-bond emission since 2020, plus a daily-scored BVB secondary-market screener.
**For:** Romanian retail savers deciding between Tezaur, Fidelis or buying bonds on the BVB.
**Sets it apart:** Full rate history in one place (RON, EUR, blood donors) + a screener that labels secondary-market bonds Oportunitate / Corect / Supraevaluat / Nelichid, refreshed automatically.
**Most impressive claim (from fun-facts.json):** rates went from 2.85% (Fidelis, Mar 2021) to 9.15% (Tezaur, Nov 2022) in 20 months.
**Visual hook:** that number counting 2.85% → 9.15%.
**Tone:** default — punchy, clean, on the site's own dark palette (#0d0f14 / #151820, accent #4f8ef7, green #34d399, Inter).
**Language:** Romanian, the site's language and audience.
**Share caption:** "Dobânzile la titlurile de stat au urcat de la 2.85% la 9.15% în 20 de luni. Am pus tot istoricul Fidelis & Tezaur (și piața secundară BVB) pe o singură pagină."
